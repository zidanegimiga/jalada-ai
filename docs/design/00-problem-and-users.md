# 00. Problem and users

Status: Draft

## The problem in one sentence
People with mixed income and many small spends never keep records, so they cannot see where their money goes, and tax season becomes a panic of rebuilding a year from bank and M-Pesa statements.

## Who we are designing for

### Persona 1: The freelancer with mixed income
- Salaried at times, freelancing at other times. Some clients deduct withholding tax and issue certificates.
- Money arrives through bank and M-Pesa. Between jobs, relatives and friends send support.
- Spends on tools for work: a laptop, data bundles, software subscriptions, courses, transport.
- Pain: at filing time, cannot tell which deposits were income, which were support, and which costs were for work. Certificates and receipts are scattered across email, WhatsApp and paper.
- This was my own situation in 2025. I am the first user.

### Persona 2: The informal business owner
- Runs a kiosk, salon, boda boda, small shop or market stall.
- Business and personal money share one M-Pesa line.
- Many small transactions every day. Rarely gets a formal receipt.
- Pain: cannot tell if the business is making money, and has no records if asked by the Kenya Revenue Authority or a lender.

### Persona 3: The careful spender on a small phone (to confirm)
- Wants to understand everyday spending on airtime, bundles, transport and food.
- May use a basic phone or a low-cost Android phone with little data.
- Pain: money disappears in small amounts with no picture of where it went.

## Jobs to be done
1. When money comes in or goes out, I want to record it in seconds, so I do not have to remember later.
2. When I record something, I want the app to work out what it was for, so I do not have to know accounting categories.
3. When the app is unsure, I want it to ask me simply, so my records stay correct.
4. At the end of a month, I want to see where my money went, split between business and personal.
5. At tax time, I want my figures grouped the way the return asks for them, with proof attached.
6. If the Kenya Revenue Authority asks about a deposit, I want to show what it was and the evidence.

## What success looks like for a user
- Capturing a transaction takes under ten seconds.
- Most items are categorised correctly without the user typing anything.
- At filing time, the user needs nothing outside the app to fill in their return.

## Out of scope for now
- Filing returns on the user's behalf (planned later, see the planning doc).
- Invoicing customers, stock management and payroll.
- Budgets and savings goals.

## Open questions
1. Is Persona 3 a real target, or a later audience?
2. Should informal business owners be served from day one, or after the freelancer flow works?
3. Which languages at launch: English only, or English and Kiswahili?
4. Are there users we should explicitly not serve, for example registered companies with accountants?
